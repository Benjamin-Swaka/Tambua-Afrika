About Tambua Afrika

Tambua Afrika is a multidisciplinary African creative company dedicated to discovering, developing, and distributing authentic African stories.

Our Vision

To be a leading African creative institution that shapes culture, nurtures talent, and positions African stories at the center of global artistic and intellectual exchange.

Our Mission

To identify, support, and amplify African creative talent by producing high-quality literary, theatrical, visual, and digital works, while building sustainable systems that empower creators, preserve culture, and generate economic value for communities.

The Creative Ecosystem

We bridge the gap between raw talent and global audiences through professional support, business systems, and technology.
