Partnerships

Collaborate with us to amplify African stories.

Tambua Afrika partners with cultural institutions, publishers, festivals, universities, and brands that share our mission of elevating African creativity. We offer co‑publishing, event sponsorship, talent scouting, and content licensing.

Why Partner With Us

  • Access to talent – Our growing community of creators across 12 African countries.
  • Curated content – Books, plays, comics, and animations ready for distribution.
  • Brand alignment – Associate with authentic African storytelling and cultural preservation.
  • Social impact – Support education, literacy, and creative entrepreneurship.

Types of Partnerships

Institutional

Cultural organisations, libraries, and academic institutions can collaborate on anthologies, residencies, or research projects.

Corporate

Brands seeking meaningful CSR or marketing campaigns can sponsor open calls, events, or digital content series.

Media & Distribution

Publishers, streaming platforms, and broadcasters can license our original works or co‑produce new projects.

Get in Touch

To discuss a partnership, email us at partnerships@tambuaafrika.com with a brief description of your organisation and goals.

We reply within 5 business days. Serious inquiries only, please.
