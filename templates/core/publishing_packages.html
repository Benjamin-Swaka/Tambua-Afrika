Publishing Packages

Professional support to bring your manuscript to the world.

Overview: Tambua Afrika Ink offers tiered publishing packages designed to suit different creator needs—from editorial feedback to full distribution. All packages are transparent, with no hidden fees.

1. Editorial Review — $150

Our senior editors read your manuscript (up to 80,000 words) and provide a detailed editorial report covering structure, character development, language, and market readiness. You also receive a one‑hour consultation call.

2. Manuscript Development — $500

For writers who need deeper involvement. Includes two rounds of developmental editing, line editing, and a final proofread. You receive annotated files and a publishing roadmap.

3. Full Publishing — $1,200

Our complete package. We handle cover design, interior layout, ISBN assignment, eBook conversion, and distribution to major online retailers (Amazon, Apple Books, etc.). You retain full rights and receive 70% royalties.

4. Premium Partnership — $2,500

For creators seeking a long‑term partner. Includes everything in Full Publishing plus dedicated marketing support, author website, book tour coordination, and priority placement in Tambua Afrika anthologies and events.
